A sample day

Eat more of

  • Daal, chana and lobia
  • Seasonal sabzi and salad
  • Eggs, chicken, fish
  • Yogurt (dahi) and milk
  • Fruit, nuts and seeds
  • Whole wheat roti, oats, brown rice

Eat less of

  • Sugary drinks and chai with lots of sugar
  • Samosa, pakora and other deep-fried food
  • Bakery items and sweets
  • White bread and excess white rice
  • Packaged chips and biscuits
  • Heavy ghee and oil in curries

Everyday habits

  • Sleep 7–9 hours.
  • Walk 30 minutes most days.
  • Drink water through the day, not all at once.
  • Eat slowly and stop when you feel 80% full.

This is general guidance, not medical advice. Consult a doctor or registered dietitian, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have an eating disorder.
